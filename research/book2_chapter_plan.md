# Somewhere in Africa — chapter plan (Book Two)

Companion to `drafts/Letters_Conflict_Somewhere_in_Africa_Blueprint.docx` (**Version 3**, 14 November 2026; adds five safeguards: keystone + small thrones, The Citizen Inside in every chapter, no remedies but not no agency, Book Three must answer Painted Reform, unnamed ≠ unrecognisable). Verified material: `research/book2_evidence_dossier.md`. Everything else here is a **lead or proposal**.

**Direction (author, 14 Nov 2026):** present tense; describe *the system*; personal sovereignty in place of state sovereignty; parliaments without power; education that does not equip; why reform inside a wrong system is wishful thinking; **no hope resolution**. End on **the gap** between the republic on paper and the republic in practice, which Book Three addresses. Real events, but **no names** in the prose (country, leader, party, person); sources go in the notes.

**Central image (proposed):** *the Painted Republic* (isomorphic mimicry: forms without function), linked to *the letter that became the word*.

Each chapter has **[Correction]** (When the Evidence Corrects the Story) and **[Gap]** (what the institution should do vs what it does). Notes restart at **1** for Book Two.

## Part I · The Throne (how power is arranged)

### 1 · Somewhere, This Morning — **Draft 1 done** (`manuscript/somewhere/ch01.js`)
- Composite dawn in an unnamed capital inside the painted republic. Sets the method: no names, real events, scene labels; *somewhere does not mean everywhere*.
- Evidence: Afrobarometer 2024 (demand vs supply). ✅
- [Correction] "Africans don't want democracy" vs 80% rejecting one-man rule.
- [Gap] The citizen's preference vs what is supplied (66% prefer democracy; 37% satisfied).

### 2 · The Republic of One Letter *(alt: The Kingdom of One Man)*
- Personal sovereignty: the office acquires a face; army, party, budget bent to keep it. Refer back to Origins Ch 9.
- Evidence: Roessler 2016 (coup-proofing, coup–civil war trap) ⚠️; ACSS term-limit evasion and coups ⚠️.
- [Correction] "Replace the leader and it ends" vs evidence that coups often follow term-limit evasion and rebuild the throne.
- [Gap] Security services loyal to the constitution vs to a person.

### 3 · NEW · The House That Says Yes
- Parliament without power: amendments for the executive, budgets it cannot change, audit reports not acted on, MPs' own pay.
- Leads: Open Budget Survey legislative-oversight scores; PEFA PI-31 (legislative scrutiny of audit reports); documented term/age-limit amendments; court rulings upholding them. ⚠️
- [Gap] What a legislature is constitutionally meant to do vs what the record shows.

### 4 · The Law That Recognises Faces
- Courts and police: strict downward, gentle upward.
- Leads: Afrobarometer trust in police/courts; ISS 2025 "Weaponising the law". ⚠️
