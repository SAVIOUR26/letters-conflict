## Part I · The Rules We Bend (recognition)

### 1 · Somewhere, This Morning *(alt: Somewhere in Africa)*
- **Scene (composite):** one morning in an unnamed city: boda boda traffic, the school run, a queue at a government office, a vendor moved on by enforcement officers.
- **Job:** set the method (unnamed places, real patterns, scene labels) and the central question. Variation, not "one Africa".
- **Evidence:** Afrobarometer: wide differences between countries in trust, democracy and civic activity; Ibrahim Index on cross-country diversity. [Verify round and figures]
- **[Correction]** "Nothing works anywhere in Africa" versus the documented variation and high civic participation.
- **[Holds]** A city service that works reliably (to be found and verified).

### 2 · The Republic of Small Exceptions *(alt: The Law That Recognised Faces)*
- **Scene:** the file that sits for months, then moves in minutes when the applicant "knows someone".
- **Mechanism:** conditional cooperation. People keep rules when they expect others to, and stop when they don't (public-goods experiments; Ostrom).
- **[Correction]** "Africans don't respect rules" versus evidence that rule-following depends on expectations and enforcement everywhere.
- Thread: social class and imitation.

### 3 · The Price of a Signature *(alt: The Price of Being Connected)*
- **Scene:** a citizen deciding whether to pay for a document that should be free.
- **Evidence:** Afrobarometer bribery questions; AU Convention on Preventing and Combating Corruption (2003/2006 — verify); auditor-general reports.
- **[Correction]** "Corruption is African culture" versus evidence that it responds to incentives, enforcement and reform.
- **[Holds]** A documented reform that cut petty bribery (e.g. digitised services; verify cases).

## Part II · The Promises We Keep Breaking (discomfort)

### 4 · A Classroom Full of Tomorrow *(alt: When Education Produced Spectators)*
- **Scene:** a family sells land or livestock for fees; the graduate comes home unsure where to start. [Author: witnessed?]
- **Evidence:** UNESCO UIS; World Bank learning poverty; household education spending; graduate outcomes (UBOS). [Verify]
- **Author's line:** *the system, not the student*: "the system has no power, so it cannot give power."
- **[Correction]** "A degree guarantees a job" *and* "graduates are lazy": both wrong.

### 5 · NEW — The Graduate and the Street *(pending author approval)*
- **Scene:** the engineer at home beside the unbuilt village road; a night robbery for a phone in a city slum. [Author]
- **Evidence:** UBOS NLFS 2021 (youth unemployment and NEET, reported as 17% and 41% for ages 18–30; verify); ILO on informality; police crime reports; kifeesi reporting (verify).
- **Author's question:** *who benefits from empty gaps?*
- **[Correction]** "The youth don't want to work" versus Uganda's Youth Opportunities Program and Liberia's STYL results (see the ideas bank).
- Solutions ("From Kifeesi to Workforce", graduate teams) are **left for Book Three**; this chapter only diagnoses.

### 6 · The Hospital of Waiting
- **Scene:** a mother's day at a health centre with no drugs in stock.
- **Evidence:** WHO Africa; universal health coverage monitoring (service coverage, financial protection); out-of-pocket spending; health-worker density.
- **[Holds]** A health system or facility reform with documented results.

### 7 · The Road Rebuilt Every Rainy Season
- **Scene:** the same road resurfaced before each election and broken after each rain (**the pothole**).
- **Evidence:** PEFA; public investment management reviews; auditor-general reports; road-maintenance budgets.
- Ideas bank: the imported lorry on the unmaintained road (local industry is left for Book Three).

## Part III · Who Holds the Pen (self-implication → responsibility)

### 8 · When the Person Becomes the Office *(alt: The Kingdom of One Man)*
- **Scene (composite):** an institution that stops working when its head is away.
- "What happens when an office acquires a face?" covers the president, the minister, the head teacher, the pastor, the founder and the chairman.
- **Evidence:** ACDEG; institutional capture (World Development Report 2017). Refer back to *Origins* Ch 9; do not repeat it.
- Thread: religion and the church.

### 9 · The Election of Small Gifts
- **Scene:** sugar, salt and cash on the eve of a vote, and the road that never comes.
- **Evidence:** Afrobarometer: strong support for democracy versus low satisfaction with how it works (verify current figures); AU observation reports; clientelism research.

### 10 · Silence, Exit, and the Common Good *(alt: The People Who Betrayed Their Future)*
- **Scene:** the best student in the class in the embassy queue; the whistle never blown.
- **Evidence:** Hirschman, *Exit, Voice, and Loyalty* (1970); Afrobarometer on migration; remittance data; whistle-blower laws.
- Thread: family and relationships.

### 11 · The Letter That Refused Disorder *(alt: A Continent of Unfinished Sentences)*
- **Scene (Reported or Witnessed):** a public servant, community or reform that held. [Verify / Author]
- **Evidence:** Afrobarometer civic participation as the counterweight.
- **Hand-off to Book Three (proposal):** *We already know what the sentence should say. The question is who will build the grammar that lets us write it.*
