# Somewhere in Africa — evidence dossier

Verified or partly verified material for Book Two. **Text rule (author, 14 Nov 2026):** the prose names no country, leader, party or private person, because *this happened somewhere in Africa*. The **notes** at the back still cite the real sources, since that is what makes the book credible and legally safe. Each item says how it may appear in the prose.

Status key: ✅ read in the source · ⚠️ from search summaries or secondary reports, verify before use.

## The system: personal sovereignty instead of state sovereignty

| Finding | Source | Status | How it can appear in the prose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Afrobarometer, 39 countries (Round 9, 2021/2023): 66% prefer democracy; 80% reject one-man rule; 78% reject one-party rule; 66% reject military rule. | Afrobarometer, *African Insights 2024: Democracy at Risk — the People’s Perspective* ([page](https://www.afrobarometer.org/feature/flagship-report/)) | ✅ | “Across thirty-nine countries surveyed, four in five people reject rule by one man.” |
| Only 45% say their country is mostly or fully democratic; only 37% are satisfied with how democracy works. ## Why reform inside a wrong system becomes wishful thinking

| Finding | Source | Status | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Isomorphic mimicry:** states adopt the outward forms of functional states (laws, agencies, organograms) without their function. When this pays, it becomes a **capability trap**: “the appearance of development activity masks the lack of functional development activity.” | Lant Pritchett, Michael Woolcock and Matt Andrews, *Looking Like a State: Techniques of Persistent Failure in State Capability for Implementation*, UNU-WIDER Working Paper 2012/63; *Journal of Development Studies* 49:1 (2013) ([PDF](https://www3.wider.unu.edu/sites/default/files/wp2012-063.pdf)) | ✅ (working paper read) | Chapter 11 “Painted Reform”, and the recurring image of the **painted republic**. |
| **Premature load bearing:** expecting young or weak systems to carry more than they can. | Same | ✅ | Chapter 11. |
